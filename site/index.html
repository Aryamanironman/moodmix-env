How it works

three pieces that fixed the sound

Real beat grid

Analysis stores beat times, downbeats and phrase cues. Tempo octaves are folded by subsampling the grid, not just the BPM number. Cues land on bar lines.

library_v2.py

Continuous surface

Eight continuous controls, not ten discrete bins. Beatmatching is automatic; the policy only chooses the blend. A parked fader and a hard cut are both scored worse than a real blend.

env_v1.py

Beat-anchored renderer

Every beat of deck B is warped onto deck A's beat, then mixed through a Linkwitz-Riley EQ, a swept state-variable filter, loudness matching and slow gain riding.

render_v2.py
